# Changelog

## 1.0.0

### Highlights
- Automatic camera switching while riding, by where you look
- Custom ranges with layers
- Smooth glide back and auto first person in tight spaces

### Details
- Defaults: third person looking down, first person straight ahead, selfie looking up
- Requires Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader and Fabric API; Mod Menu optional
- Known issue: a quick mouse flick across a range border can flash the other view
